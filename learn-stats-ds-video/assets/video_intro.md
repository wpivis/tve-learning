Now, you will read some text and watch several instructional videos to learn the key concepts. Learning goals of this activity are to understand:

- The lower bound and upper bound of a confidence interval indicate the range of β1s that we would consider *likely* to have produced the sample b1s.
- A 95% confidence interval means that we can be 95% confident that the true β1 is somewhere between the two boundaries.
- Use the confidence interval to reason about the relationship between two variables.
